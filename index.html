What's inside

built for slowing down

Plume 3D is small on purpose. Every feature exists to remove friction between you and the calm of getting absorbed in something pretty.

🎨

curated palettes

Hand-picked color sets that always look intentional. Plus five custom slots in the main window that you can change endlessly.

🎧

lo-fi music, built in

Soft loops play while you paint. The kind of background that makes problems disappear.

✋

immersive experience

Pinch, rotate, tap. The whole scene rotates — paint the back of the lighthouse if you want.

🕯️

three lighting moods

Day, golden hour, night. Same scene, different vibe at every hour.

🖼️

your gallery

Every painting saved. A small archive of you, growing.

🌱

optional challenges

If you feel like a challenge, there are bigger models to color. If not — no pressure.

Lighting

three moods, one scene

The same little room, lit three ways. Switch the light however you feel today.
